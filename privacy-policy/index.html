PRIVACY NOTICE

How MultiPost handles connected-platform data.

Effective date: 18 October 2026. TIGO operates the MultiPost product website and Windows application. This notice explains the data handling for this website and for a supported connection, including the Google and YouTube upload connection.

Contact: for privacy questions, local-data deletion help, or a request concerning support correspondence, email TIGO at ovtom22@gmail.com.

1. Scope

This notice applies to MultiPost, its product website, and the data handled by a released or testing build when a creator uses a supported platform connection. Platform availability can differ by build, account eligibility, provider approval, and the creator's selected destination. This notice does not replace the privacy terms of Google, YouTube, or another connected platform.

2. Information MultiPost handles

Depending on the features a creator uses, MultiPost can handle the following information:

  • Selected media file paths, file metadata, prepared local delivery variants, cover selections, titles, descriptions, captions, scheduling choices, and publishing settings.
  • Connected-account display names, platform identifiers, connection status, publishing timestamps, remote post or video identifiers, and platform response messages.
  • Access and refresh credentials returned after the creator completes a supported authorization flow.
  • Local app preferences, drafts, job history, and operational log messages.
  • The sender's email address and message content when the sender contacts TIGO by email.

3. Google and YouTube data

When the YouTube connection is available in a MultiPost build and the creator chooses to connect it, MultiPost opens the Google authorization page in the system browser. The creator signs in directly with Google and chooses whether to grant the requested access. MultiPost does not ask for or receive the creator's Google password.

The YouTube connection requests these Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, used to upload the creator-selected video and, where selected, its thumbnail.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl, used because MultiPost can upload a creator-selected caption track.

After authorization, MultiPost uses the returned credentials to identify the connected YouTube channel, upload the video chosen by the creator, upload the selected thumbnail, and upload a caption track when the creator selects one. The YouTube upload can include the video's title, description, privacy setting, and any selected captions. Google and YouTube process data sent to their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Local storage and credential protection

MultiPost stores its working records in the current Windows user's application-data folder, normally %APPDATA%\MultiPost. The app database keeps local drafts, job history, connected-account records, and the paths to selected files. The selected source video remains on the creator's computer unless the creator directs MultiPost to send it to a connected platform.

For the YouTube desktop OAuth connection, access and refresh credentials are stored in the local app database protected with Windows Data Protection API for the current Windows user. The YouTube authorization and token exchange use Google's services directly. The commercial YouTube flow does not send the creator's Google access or refresh credential through a TIGO authorization broker.

5. How information is used and shared

MultiPost uses the data above to show the selected account, prepare and review a creator-selected post, make the platform request the creator approves, report the platform response, and provide support when contacted. MultiPost does not use YouTube API data to serve advertising or to build an advertising audience.

When the creator connects or publishes, the relevant information is sent to the selected platform under the creator's direction. For the YouTube connection, this can include Google authorization information and the video, thumbnail, captions, title, description, and publishing settings needed by YouTube. If the creator enables an optional AI writing feature, the text supplied for that feature can be sent to the provider selected for that request.

The current product website is static and does not include an analytics script, advertising script, payment form, or visitor-account registration.

6. Disconnecting, revocation, and deletion

A creator can disconnect a YouTube account from MultiPost. For a YouTube OAuth connection, MultiPost first attempts to send a revocation request to Google and then clears the local sign-in record. If Google cannot confirm the revocation request, MultiPost still clears the local sign-in record and reports that condition to the creator.

To remove local MultiPost data, close the application and remove the %APPDATA%\MultiPost folder. This removes local drafts, history, settings, and stored connection records from that Windows user profile. It does not remove videos, channels, posts, or records held by YouTube or another platform. Those must be managed through the relevant platform account and its privacy controls.

To request deletion of support correspondence held by TIGO, email ovtom22@gmail.com from the address used to contact support and describe the request. TIGO does not state a separate server-side retention period for YouTube credentials because the YouTube connection described here stores them locally in the creator's Windows profile.

7. Security and account responsibility

MultiPost is designed to use official provider authorization pages and to protect locally stored connection credentials with the current Windows user's profile protection. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Creators should protect their Windows account and connected-platform accounts and should not send passwords, access tokens, developer credentials, or local database files to support.

8. Google policies and changes

Google's handling of data is governed by Google's own policies, including the Google Privacy Policy and the Google API Services User Data Policy. MultiPost may update this notice when the data flow or available features change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated with the notice.
