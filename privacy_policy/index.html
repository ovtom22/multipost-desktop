Privacy Policy · Last updated: 28 August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the MultiPost Desktop Windows application and this official website.

Data stored on your computer

Login tokens, captions, job history, and settings are stored locally in your Windows user profile (%APPDATA%\MultiPost). They are not uploaded to a MultiPost Desktop server. MultiPost Desktop does not operate a cloud backend for posting.

TikTok

When you click Log in, MultiPost Desktop opens the official TikTok sign-in page. After you approve, TikTok returns access tokens so the app can publish videos you choose. We request user.info.basic, video.upload, and video.publish. We do not read your TikTok followers, messages, or For You feed.

Videos

Videos stay on your disk until you publish. Then they are sent only to the networks you selected (for example the TikTok Content Posting API).

Optional AI rewrite

If you paste an AI API key in Settings, titles and captions you ask to rewrite are sent to that provider. Leave the key empty to keep rewrites local.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share tokens with third parties except the social network you logged into, as required to publish.
