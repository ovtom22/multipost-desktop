TIKTOK CONTENT POSTING FLOW

A creator reviews the post before the video leaves the computer.

MultiPost is designed to support authentic creators publishing content they control. It does not present TikTok as an automatic destination for content copied from other networks.

  1. Connect the creator's own account

    Opening TikTok sign-in takes the creator to TikTok's official authorization experience. The desktop app then loads the current account information and post options allowed by TikTok for that account.

  2. Select original or rights-cleared content

    The creator chooses a video from the local computer and reviews it before upload. The available duration and media restrictions are checked against the current TikTok account settings.

  3. Choose the audience and disclosures explicitly

    The TikTok review shows the creator identity and the privacy choices returned by TikTok. No audience is preselected. Comment, Duet, and Stitch begin disabled and remain unavailable when TikTok does not allow them. Commercial or AI-content disclosures are selected when applicable.

  4. Confirm the reviewed post

    Before transfer, the creator reviews the selected account, video, caption, audience, settings, and platform terms, then gives explicit posting consent. Changing material details requires a fresh review.

  5. Check the saved receipt before trying again

    After TikTok receives an upload, processing can continue. The activity queue can check the saved upload receipt without creating another upload. A successful upload is not represented as a public post until TikTok reports the relevant outcome.

TIKTOK ACCESS

Only the scopes used by the publishing flow

MultiPost uses TikTok Login Kit with user.info.basic to display the current creator account, plus video.upload and video.publish for a video the creator selects and approves.

NOT PART OF THIS FLOW

No feed, follower, or message reading

The flow does not request the creator's For You feed, follower list, direct messages, or other users' content. Current-account information keeps the publishing decision visible to the creator.

WHAT MULTIPOST DOES

Maintains a review boundary

The app preserves an explicit review and consent step between local media preparation and the TikTok publishing action.

WHAT MULTIPOST DOES NOT CLAIM

It cannot override TikTok

TikTok controls account eligibility, moderation, visibility, processing time, and production approval. The application does not guarantee that a post will be public or receive distribution.

CURRENT REVIEW STATUS

TikTok production approval is not claimed here.

Until TikTok approves Direct Post for production, TikTok limits unaudited clients to private accounts and Only you visibility. The eventual public website, product demo, app configuration, and release behavior must match the approved scope.
