MultiPost Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

MultiPost is a Windows desktop application. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we store on your computer

Login tokens, captions, job history, and settings are stored locally in your Windows user profile (%APPDATA%\MultiPost). They are not uploaded to a MultiPost server. MultiPost does not operate a cloud backend for posting.

TikTok and other networks

When you click Log in, MultiPost opens the official TikTok (or other network) sign-in page. After you approve, TikTok returns access tokens so the app can publish videos you choose. We request user.info.basic, video.upload, and video.publish for TikTok. We do not read your TikTok followers, messages, or For You feed.

Videos

Videos stay on your disk until you publish. Then they are sent only to the networks you selected (for example TikTok Content Posting API).

Optional AI rewrite

If you paste an AI API key in Settings, titles and captions you ask to rewrite are sent to that provider. Leave the key empty to keep rewrites local.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share tokens with third parties except the social network you logged into, as required to publish.

Contact

Questions: the email on your TikTok for Developers account.
