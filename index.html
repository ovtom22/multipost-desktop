A REVIEWABLE WORKFLOW

A video is not a publish button.

Each destination can have different rules, limits, account permissions, captions, and visibility choices. MultiPost keeps those decisions visible before any media is sent.

01 / PREPARE

Build a platform-ready copy

Use a local source file, inspect it, and prepare supported delivery variants without losing track of the original.

02 / REVIEW

See what each platform needs

Review caption, cover, account, destination settings, warnings, and consent separately before publishing.

03 / FOLLOW THROUGH

Read the result without guessing

The activity view separates a submitted upload from processing, a confirmed post, or a state that needs your attention.

STATUS WITH CONTEXT
